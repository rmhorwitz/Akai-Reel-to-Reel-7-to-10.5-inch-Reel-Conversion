# Akai-Reel-to-Reel-7-to-10.5-inch-Reel-Conversion
This repository contains drawings, 3D models, BOMs, and instructions for converting a 7" reel Akai machine to a 10.5"
I recently picked up two Akai GX 285D reel to reel tape machines for a great deal. Both units only support 7" reels and I thought it would be cool if I could convert both units to 10.5" reels. I did a bit of research and found that Akai had once sold a conversion kit called the Akai EA-10 that converted a R2R to accept larger NAB 10.5" reels using pair of outriggers, pulleys and belts. I saw someone have one of these kits for sale for an attractive price, as one of the pulley mounts was defective. So, I bought it and gave it a try. Long story short, the Akai EA-10 kit is applicable to a small number of older R2R that were made back in the mid 60's. Here is the list:

Akai 345

Akai X-355

Akai X-355D

Roberts 400 (Roberts was the US brand name for Akai-manufactured units)

Roberts 445

Akai 33 / 220

Only a handful of these units survive today and it is doubtful that the owner's are seeking out accessories rather than spare parts to keep them functional. So these EA-10 kits are about as valuable as a stale fruit cake during the holiday season. However, not to be defeated that easy, I did some engineering and updated the design to accept modified pulleys compatible for newer Akai tape machines. I designed an adapter plate for the outriggers which were initially designed to fit directly on the spindles motor mounts. These outriggers now attached to the front panel sheet metal where they are securely fastened. I looked at a few Akai R2R models on ebay (they made over 100) and identified models that would be compatible with this modified kit. Here is a partial list:

GX-220D

GX-230D

GX-250D

GX-270D

GX-280D

GX-285D

There could be several more models. They all need to have a RTMA trident hub that is secured by three screws and a flat front panel.

Installing the kit

On my GX-280/285D, there is a thin ring bezel that is glued around the hub pulley on the front panel. This bezel is removed from the front panel. The front panel is removed so that the outriggers and adapter plates can be installed (NO DRILLING!!!). Both outrigger arms are situated and fully secured. Next the RTMA trident hubs and spacers are removed by removing the three screws. The new kit pulley hubs are installed and secured using the same 3 screws. The front panel (with the outriggers installed and secured) is placed back on to the unit chassis and fastened back together. The rubber belts are installed and the left and right pulley mounts are adjusted as desired. That is it!!!
